# ProjectionWorld

ProjectionWorld is the controlled semantic environment experienced by the observer.

It is one of the central architectural objects of ASD.

ProjectionWorld is not:
- a collection of fake responses
- a static honeypot
- an isolated simulation
- a scripted deception tree

ProjectionWorld is a persistent semantic reality layer maintained for defensive interaction.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of ProjectionWorld is to maintain a coherent observable environment capable of shaping the observer's evolving hypothesis state.

ProjectionWorld exists to:
- preserve semantic continuity
- support controlled discovery
- maintain believable environmental structure
- constrain observer interpretation
- provide adaptive defensive flexibility

---

## Core Principle

ProjectionWorld is not optimized for realism alone.

It is optimized for:
- perceived coherence
- adaptive plausibility
- observer-state stability
- controlled semantic evolution

The objective is not to perfectly imitate reality.

The objective is to maintain a defensible and semantically stable perceived reality.

---

## Projection Layers

ProjectionWorld may contain multiple interacting semantic layers.

Examples include:
- infrastructure appearance
- service topology
- artifact relationships
- operational history
- metadata continuity
- temporal consistency
- environmental imperfections
- apparent organizational structure

These layers collectively form the observer's perceived environment.

---

## Persistence

ProjectionWorld must maintain persistence across interactions.

An observer should experience:
- continuity
- state retention
- environmental memory
- stable semantic references
- progressive exploration

Projection instability increases detection probability.

---

## Controlled Discovery

ProjectionWorld is designed around gradual discovery.

Information exposure must be:
- paced
- bounded
- strategically controlled
- context-aware

Overexposure reduces projection credibility.

The observer should feel:
- progression
- uncertainty
- partial understanding
- evolving confidence

---

## Semantic Continuity

Every interaction modifies ProjectionWorld.

All emitted artifacts become part of future consistency requirements.

This includes:
- naming conventions
- topology
- timing patterns
- historical traces
- environmental logic
- discovered structures

ProjectionWorld must remain semantically coherent over time.

---

## Controlled Imperfection

Perfect environments are often suspicious.

ProjectionWorld intentionally permits:
- bounded inconsistency
- operational noise
- stale artifacts
- incomplete structures
- timing irregularities
- human-like errors

Imperfection improves perceived realism.

---

## Observer Coupling

ProjectionWorld adapts to the observer's reconstructed hypothesis state.

Different observers may experience:
- different pacing
- different structures
- different artifact visibility
- different semantic paths
- different ambiguity levels

ProjectionWorld is observer-relative.

---

## Defensive Constraints

ProjectionWorld is bounded by:
- SafetyBoundary
- RealityBoundary
- ExposureBudget
- semantic consistency constraints
- defensive operational limits

ProjectionWorld must never expose:
- real infrastructure
- real operational topology
- privileged information
- uncontrolled escalation paths

---

## Projection Drift

Projection Drift occurs when semantic consistency degrades over time.

Possible causes include:
- excessive artifact generation
- inconsistent pacing
- uncontrolled adaptation
- observer-state reconstruction failure
- continuity mismatch

Projection Drift increases:
- suspicion
- instability
- observer confidence loss
- detection probability

Preventing Projection Drift is a core architectural responsibility.

---

## Adaptive Projection

ProjectionWorld is adaptive rather than static.

It may:
- reinforce assumptions
- redirect exploration
- reduce certainty
- increase ambiguity
- delay discovery
- contain escalation
- stabilize observer expectations

Adaptation must remain semantically coherent.

## Strategic Goal

The strategic objective of ProjectionWorld is not deception for its own sake.

The objective is controlled defensive interaction through semantic environment management.

ProjectionWorld attempts to shape:
- what the observer perceives
- how the observer interprets
- how quickly conclusions evolve
- which structures appear meaningful
- which paths remain visible

The observer experiences a world.

ASD manages the semantic conditions of that world.
