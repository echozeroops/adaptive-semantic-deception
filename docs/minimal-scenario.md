# Minimal Scenario

This document presents a simplified conceptual interaction scenario inside an ASD-managed environment.

The goal is not protocol realism.

The goal is to demonstrate observer-state evolution inside a controlled ProjectionWorld.

---

## Scenario Overview

An external observer begins exploring an exposed environment believed to contain operational infrastructure.

The environment is managed by ASD.

ASD does not immediately block interaction.

Instead, the system attempts to:
- observe behavior
- reconstruct assumptions
- maintain semantic continuity
- shape exploration
- slow escalation
- extend defensive reaction time

---

## Initial Observer Action

The observer initiates low-confidence exploratory interaction.

Example actions may include:
- service probing
- directory exploration
- metadata inspection
- structure validation
- timing observation

At this stage:
- observer certainty is low
- assumptions are unstable
- ProjectionWorld exposure remains minimal

---

## ObserverModel Initialization

ASD creates an initial ObserverModel.

The system begins estimating:
- apparent intent
- exploration style
- confidence level
- pacing behavior
- structural assumptions
- suspicion indicators

The observer is treated as an evolving cognitive process rather than a static source of events.

---

## Initial ProjectionWorld Exposure

ProjectionWorld reveals only limited semantic structure.

The observer experiences:
- partial visibility
- incomplete environmental understanding
- low-confidence discovery
- plausible operational noise
- controlled ambiguity

ExposureBudget remains conservative.

---

## Hypothesis Reconstruction

ASD attempts to estimate what the observer currently believes.

Possible assumptions may include:
- infrastructure type
- organizational structure
- system importance
- environment stability
- hidden asset existence

This estimated state becomes the ReconstructedHypothesis.

---

## Adaptive Interaction

As interaction continues, ASD:
- adjusts pacing
- modifies visibility
- introduces artifacts
- preserves continuity
- maintains uncertainty
- delays confident interpretation

The observer experiences gradual environmental evolution rather than static deception.

---

## Controlled Imperfection

ProjectionWorld intentionally contains:
- stale references
- incomplete structures
- operational inconsistencies
- environmental noise
- imperfect timing behavior

These imperfections improve perceived realism.

---

## Suspicion Management

ASD continuously evaluates:
- escalation tempo
- confidence accumulation
- probing intensity
- inconsistency sensitivity
- observer adaptation patterns

If suspicion increases:
- exposure pacing may slow
- ambiguity may increase
- discovery paths may narrow
- projection adaptation may stabilize

---

## Defensive Time Acquisition

One of the primary operational goals of ASD is defensive time acquisition.

By sustaining controlled semantic interaction, ASD attempts to:
- delay confident escalation
- consume adversarial attention
- increase exploratory cost
- extend defensive reaction windows
- support containment and analysis

Interaction itself becomes a defensive resource.

---

## Projection Continuity

Every emitted artifact modifies future ProjectionWorld requirements.

ASD must preserve:
- semantic continuity
- topology consistency
- naming coherence
- plausible environmental evolution
- bounded adaptation

Projection instability increases detection probability.

---

## DecisionTrace Logging

All major projection decisions are recorded through DecisionTrace.

This enables:
- explainability
- post-event analysis
- consistency auditing
- observer-path reconstruction
- defensive evaluation

---

## Scenario End State

The objective of ASD is not necessarily to convince the observer completely.

The objective is to maintain:
- controlled uncertainty
- bounded perception
- delayed escalation
- defensive stability
- semantically coherent interaction

ASD attempts to shape the observer's evolving model of the environment while remaining inside strict defensive boundaries.
