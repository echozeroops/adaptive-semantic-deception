# Decision Model

The ASD decision model is responsible for selecting semantically coherent defensive actions based on the observer's reconstructed cognitive state.

ASD does not treat decisions as isolated responses.

Each decision becomes part of an evolving semantic environment.

---

## Core Principle

The ASD decision process attempts to answer:

> What decision best shapes the observer's evolving hypothesis state while remaining semantically coherent and defensively bounded?

The objective is not merely response generation.

The objective is controlled observer-state evolution.

---

## Decision Inputs

ASD decisions may incorporate:

- ObserverModel state
- ReconstructedHypothesis
- ProjectionWorld consistency
- ExposureBudget status
- semantic continuity constraints
- suspicion indicators
- exploration pacing
- artifact history
- strategic containment objectives
- defensive operational limits

No single input fully determines system behavior.

Decisions emerge from the interaction of multiple semantic constraints.

---

## Decision Layers

### Observer Interpretation Layer

Estimates:
- what the observer currently believes
- how stable those beliefs appear
- what assumptions are being tested
- what uncertainty remains

### Projection Evaluation Layer

Evaluates:
- current ProjectionWorld stability
- semantic consistency
- artifact coherence
- pacing quality
- continuity preservation

### Strategy Selection Layer

Determines the most appropriate semantic direction.

Possible strategic actions include:
- reinforcement
- ambiguity increase
- exploration redirection
- delayed disclosure
- controlled contradiction
- escalation containment
- confidence shaping

### Safety Validation Layer

Validates all candidate actions against:
- SafetyBoundary
- RealityBoundary
- exposure constraints
- operational safety requirements

Unsafe actions must be rejected regardless of strategic value.

### Artifact Emission Layer

Generates observable outputs capable of influencing observer perception.

Every emitted artifact modifies future consistency requirements.

---

## Observer-State Evolution

ASD assumes that observer cognition evolves over time.

The decision model therefore attempts to influence:
- confidence accumulation
- interpretation direction
- perceived structure
- exploration depth
- uncertainty persistence
- escalation tempo

The observer is treated as a continuously adapting cognitive process.

---

## Semantic Coherence

Every ASD decision must preserve semantic coherence.

This includes consistency across:
- naming
- timing
- structure
- topology
- operational logic
- historical traces
- environmental state

Semantically inconsistent decisions increase detection probability.

---

## Decision Persistence

Decisions are not isolated events.

Every decision contributes to:
- ProjectionWorld evolution
- observer expectations
- future artifact interpretation
- continuity requirements
- strategic trajectory

ASD therefore treats decisions as stateful semantic operations.

---

## Adaptive Pacing

The decision model continuously evaluates pacing quality.

Pacing determines:
- how quickly structures appear
- how rapidly certainty evolves
- how much information becomes visible
- how aggressively exploration progresses

Poor pacing destabilizes ProjectionWorld.

---

## Suspicion Management

ASD attempts to avoid:
- excessive consistency
- excessive perfection
- abrupt adaptation
- unrealistic disclosure density
- semantic overfitting

The system intentionally preserves bounded uncertainty and controlled imperfection.

Suspicion management is treated as a first-class architectural concern.

---

## Decision Constraints

The ASD decision model must never:
- expose real infrastructure
- reveal protected operational assets
- lose ProjectionWorld continuity
- bypass SafetyBoundary constraints
- create uncontrolled escalation
- generate semantically impossible environments

Safety constraints always override strategic objectives.

---

## Minimal Decision Loop

```text
Observer Interaction
        ↓
ObserverModel Update
        ↓
Hypothesis Reconstruction
        ↓
ProjectionWorld Evaluation
        ↓
Strategy Selection
        ↓
Safety Validation
        ↓
Artifact Emission
        ↓
DecisionTrace Logging
```

---

## Explainability

All ASD decisions should remain explainable through DecisionTrace.

The system should support:
- post-event analysis
- consistency auditing
- projection reconstruction
- strategic evaluation
- observer-path analysis

Explainability is necessary for maintaining controlled defensive behavior.

---

## Strategic Objective

The ASD decision model exists to maintain:

- semantic stability
- observer-state control
- adaptive defensive flexibility
- bounded environmental projection
- defensive time acquisition
- controlled escalation management

The goal is not automated deception alone.

The goal is controlled defensive cognition management inside adaptive semantic environments.
